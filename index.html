Phase 02

Time is an
Ingredient.

Unlike industrial ghee made directly from raw cream, we follow the authentic Ayurvedic tradition. The fresh milk is gently boiled over a wood-fire and inoculated with a natural starter culture, transforming it into probiotic-rich curd (Dahi) overnight.

Phase 03

The Wooden
Bilona.

During the ambrosial hours of Brahma Muhurta (before sunrise), the curd is hand-churned in both directions using a traditional wooden bilona. This bi-directional movement separates the rich, creamy Makhan (butter) while infusing the positive energy of the process.

Phase 04

Forged in
Warmth.

Slow-simmered over a gentle, open cow-dung flame. As the heat rises, the liquid clarifies, revealing a golden, nutrient-dense nectar with an unmistakable, roasted aroma and crystalline texture.
